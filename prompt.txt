Build a near-identical clone of Karthikeya Finance Management, a React Native / Expo field-agent app for weekly micro-loan collection across villages.

Project overview:
Use React Native with Expo SDK 54, TypeScript, expo-router, Firebase Auth, and Cloud Firestore. The live web app is karthikeya-finance.web.app. The app is used by a field finance agent to organize villages by day and shift, register customers, disburse micro-loans, collect weekly payments, mark dues, renew loans, export reports, and monitor funds.

Color palette:
Primary Blue #1565C0, Dark Navy #0D1B2A, Accent #1976D2, Light Tint #E3F2FD, Green #2E7D32, Red #C62828, Orange #C55A11, Purple/PhonePe #5F259F, Card BG #F5F9FF, Text Secondary #546E7A. Main gradient: 135deg from #1565C0 to #0D1B2A.

Screens:
Login and Signup (`app/(01)/login.tsx`, `app/(01)/index.tsx`): email/password auth, Google sign-in, forgot password.
Dashboard/Shift Selection (`app/(02)/shift-selection.tsx`): Mon-Sun day picker, Morning/Evening shift picker, today collection/disbursement cards.
Village List (`app/village/[day]/[shift].tsx`): add/delete villages scoped to selected day and shift.
Customer List (`app/customer/[villageId].tsx`): searchable customer cards, stats bar filtered by search, GPS actions, document status, dual pay buttons: Cash blue and PhonePe purple. Payment modal defaults from tapped button and lets user switch mode.
Add/Edit Customer: name, phone, Aadhaar, GPS/location description, co-applicant, auto book number, principal, registration date, submitted documents, and loan disbursement mode Cash/PhonePe.
Customer Profile (`app/profile/[customerId].tsx`): profile details, loan status, repayment progress, civil score, payment history, payment edit/delete, DUE delete, disbursement mode badge, PAY/DUE/RENEW flows.
Payment Modal: unified payment entry for Cash/PhonePe; mode toggle at top; saves payment `type` as CASH or PHONE while retaining existing compatibility fields; DUE saves type DUE.
Reports (`app/(03)/reports.tsx`): date range reports with Excel/PDF/image style exports and private-field exclusions.
Analytics (`app/(04)/graph.tsx`): financial dashboard with collections, disbursements, investments, expenses, dues, recent payments, wallet balances, and charts.
Accounts/Settings (`app/(08)/account.tsx`): balancing fund, wallet opening balances, running Cash/PhonePe balances, private notes, investments, expenses, export controls.

Data models:
UserProfile: id, userId, email, accountNotes?, cashOpeningBalance?, phonePeOpeningBalance?, walletOpeningDate?.
Village: id, name, dayOfWeek, shift, userId.
Customer: id, numericalId, name, phone, aadhar, locationDesc, latitude?, longitude?, aadharSubmitted?, passportPhotoSubmitted?, coName?, coId?, villageId, userId, isActive, createdAt.
Loan: id, customerId, principalAmount, interestAmount, totalPayable, balanceAmount, userId, startDate, status ACTIVE/CLOSED/RENEWED, disbursement_mode? CASH/PHONE.
Payment: id, loanId, customerId?, amountPaid, paymentDate, weekNumber, paymentType REGULAR/DUE/RENEWAL_CLOSURE, paymentMode CASH/PHONE, type CASH/PHONE/DUE, notes?, userId.
Expense: id, userId, amount, description, date, payment_mode? CASH/PHONE.
Investment: id, userId, amount, date, investorName?, payment_mode? CASH/PHONE.

Firestore schema:
`users/{userId}` stores private account notes and wallet opening fields.
`villages/{id}` stores route organization.
`customers/{id}` stores customer identity, documents, location, and route ownership.
`loans/{id}` stores principal/interest/payable/balance/status/disbursement mode.
`payments/{id}` stores collections, dues, renewal closures, and payment mode/type.
`expenses/{id}` and `investments/{id}` store operational outflows by wallet mode.
`balancingFund/{id}` stores legacy/current fund overrides.
`blockedAadhaar/{id}` stores blocked Aadhaar records.

Business rules:
Interest is 20%: `principal * 1.2 = totalPayable`.
Cash actually handed to the customer is reduced by Rs.20 per Rs.1,000 of principal:
`calculateDisbursedAmount(loanAmount) = loanAmount - (Math.floor(loanAmount / 1000) * 20)`.
Loan principal, interest, total payable, and repayment balance stay based on the full principal. Wallet deductions, disbursed totals, and route/dashboard distribution summaries use the reduced cash-to-hand amount.
Book number resets per village/day/shift route.
Civil score starts at 600, adds +10 for on-time payment, subtracts -30 for DUE, clamped 300-900. Labels: Poor <600, Good 600-750, Excellent >750.
Excel colors: Orange #C55A11 for new loans, black for payments, red fill + white text for dues, purple #5F259F for remaining balance. Apply 2% deduction on total disbursed column.
Payment modes: CASH #1565C0, PHONE #5F259F, DUE #C62828.
Disbursement modes: CASH default for old loans, PHONE for PhonePe disbursement.
Wallet balance uses walletOpeningDate as cutoff. Cash loan deductions and PhonePe loan deductions use the reduced cash-to-hand disbursement amount, not full principal. Cash = cashOpeningBalance - cash disbursements + cash payments - cash expenses + cash investments. PhonePe = phonePeOpeningBalance - PhonePe disbursements + PhonePe payments - PhonePe expenses + PhonePe investments.

DUE entries are zero-amount ledger marks. The profile ledger delete action supports regular payments and DUE entries. Deleting a regular payment restores the loan balance; deleting a DUE entry removes only the payment document and does not change loan balance.

Export rules:
Include operational loan/payment/report data. Never export accountNotes, cashOpeningBalance, phonePeOpeningBalance, walletOpeningDate, or any field marked `// PRIVATE — never export`. Hide private UI with an export flag before screenshot/PDF capture when needed.

Architecture:
Use MVVM-style screen hooks and repository helpers. All Firestore queries must filter by userId. Realtime listeners must unsubscribe in `useEffect` cleanup. Use expo-router file-based navigation. Keep derived financial calculations in memoized utilities.

UI specs:
Use 16dp cards, 24dp modals, elevation 4/shadowOpacity 0.1, 20-24px bold headers, 14-16px body text, and Indian rupee formatting (`₹1,23,456` or `Rs.1,23,456` consistently with local screen style). Prefer skeleton loading states. Dual payment buttons are side by side and equal width: Cash #1565C0, PhonePe #5F259F.
